# Venture Pitch Outline — FoodFast RSU

## 1. Title Slide & Hook
* **Title:** FoodFast RSU — Zero-Queue Campus Meal Pre-Ordering
* **Tagline:** Skip the line, save your time.
* **Hook:** "Why waste 20 minutes in line for a 10-minute lunch?"

## 2. Problem Statement & User Evidence
* **Context:** RSU students at Building 6 and 11 canteens have 15-minute breaks between lectures.
* **Pain Point:** Congested canteen stalls lead to missed meals or tardiness to exams.

## 3. Product Solution & Interactive Demo
* **Solution:** Web-based pre-ordering platform requiring zero app installation.
* **Core Capabilities:** 2-click meal selection, guaranteed 12-minute prep time, real-time status tracking, and vendor queue dashboard[cite: 1].

## 4. Business Model & Go-To-Market
* **Monetization:** ฿5 campus transaction convenience fee per order + optional vendor featured listing fees.
* **GTM Strategy:** QR code poster placements on classroom doors, LINE official accounts, and Instagram student promotions[cite: 2].

## 5. Startup Metrics & Validation Results
* **Landing Page Conversion:** 620 total views, 48.3% CTA click-through rate, 31.2% checkout conversion[cite: 2].
* **Usability Score:** 4.7 / 5.0 satisfaction across student and vendor testing sessions[cite: 4].
* **Fulfillment Performance:** Average cycle time reduced to 11.5 minutes[cite: 1].
