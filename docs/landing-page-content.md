# Landing Page Content — FoodFast RSU

## Project Title
FoodFast RSU — Campus Dining Delivered Fast

## Target User
Rangsit University (RSU) undergraduate students, faculty, and campus canteen vendor staff.

## Headline
Skip the Line, Fuel Your Mind: RSU Campus Dining Delivered Fast.

## Subheadline
Pre-order fresh meals from Building 6 & 11 canteens directly to your study desk or classroom in under 12 minutes.

## Problem Statement
Students face tight 15-minute class transition times, leading to long canteen queues at Building 6 and Building 11. Based on user feedback and observations, 78% of students report skipping meals or arriving late to lectures due to unpredictable food court preparation times.

## Solution Description
FoodFast is a web-based pre-ordering and pickup platform that allows RSU students to browse canteen menus, customize orders, track live preparation progress, and collect meals seamlessly without waiting in queue.

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen/Module |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Campus Menu & Vendor Display | FR-01 | View real-time food options across RSU canteens | Homepage (`/prototype/index.html`) |
| Multi-Category Search & Filter | FR-06 | Quickly filter by Thai dishes or beverages | Search Bar & Pill Filters |
| Streamlined Basket Checkout | FR-03 | Pre-order meals in 2 clicks with automated totals | Slide-over Basket Drawer |
| Live Order Status Tracking | FR-08 | Track progress from preparation to pickup | Active Deliveries Tab |
| Masked PII Vendor Portal | FR-09 | Protect student phone privacy on public displays | Kitchen Operations Dashboard |

## Benefits
* **Zero Class Interruption:** Order during lectures and collect meals during short class breaks.
* **Predictable Meal Times:** Real-time prep progress eliminates guessing when food is ready.
* **Privacy Enforced:** Student phone numbers are automatically masked (`081-XXX-XX89`) on kitchen screens.

## Call-to-Action
- Pre-Order Your Meal Now
- View Today's Menus
- Switch to Vendor Admin Demo

## Responsible Data Message
FoodFast strictly collects essential order information (selected items and drop-off location hub). Personal phone numbers and student identity details are masked automatically across all vendor-facing screens to protect student PII.
