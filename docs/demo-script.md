# Live Demonstration Script — FoodFast RSU

## Demo Overview
* **Presenter:** Arkar & Team
* **Target Audience:** Course Instructors, Venture Investors, and RSU Canteen Management
* **Total Duration:** 3 Minutes

## Step-by-Step Demonstration Script

| Time | Presenter Action | Screen / Visual | Feature / Requirement ID | Script / What to Say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:30** | Show Landing Page | Promotional Landing Page (`/landing-page/index.html`) | `FR-01`, `FR-13` | "Welcome to FoodFast RSU. During peak hours, RSU students waste over 20 minutes in canteen queues. Our platform lets students pre-order meals in under 12 minutes." |
| **0:30 - 1:15** | Click CTA & Search | Web Prototype Homepage (`/prototype/index.html`) | `FR-02`, `FR-05`, `FR-06` | "By clicking 'Pre-Order Now', we enter the active menu. Students can search for items like 'Holy Basil' or filter by category pills like 'Thai Dishes' or 'Beverages'." |
| **1:15 - 1:45** | Add to Basket & Checkout | Slide-over Basket Drawer | `FR-03`, `FR-04`, `FR-10`, `FR-11` | "Selecting an item opens our instant basket drawer. Subtotals and campus delivery fees are calculated automatically. Clicking 'Confirm Order' submits the request instantly." |
| **1:45 - 2:15** | Track Order Progress | Active Deliveries Status Tab | `FR-07`, `FR-08` | "Students track their live order progress—from 'Preparing Meal' to 'Ready for Pickup'—eliminating idle waiting time at the food stall." |
| **2:15 - 3:00** | Switch to Vendor Admin | Kitchen Operations Dashboard | `FR-09`, `FR-12`, `FR-15` | "On the vendor side, canteen staff manage live incoming kitchen queues. Student phone numbers are automatically masked (`081-XXX-XX89`) to preserve privacy." |
