# Digital Go-to-Market Plan — FoodFast RSU

## Product / Prototype Name
FoodFast RSU — Campus Dining Pre-order Web App

## Target Early Users
RSU undergraduate students attending lectures in Building 11 (Digital Media Hub) and Building 6 who regularly order lunch during peak hours (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM).

## Main Value Proposition
Skip canteen queues and get fresh campus meals ready for pickup in under 12 minutes.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Class LINE Chat Groups | RSU Students | Save time between lectures with pre-ordering | Click demo link | Link clicks & Cart conversions | Students actively check LINE during lectures |
| Campus Poster QR Codes | Students at Canteens | Skip the 20-minute Kra Pao line | Scan QR code | QR scans & Landing visits | Placed directly where queue friction occurs |
| Instagram Stories (@RSU_FoodFast) | RSU Student Body | Convenient campus food utility | Swipe up / Click bio link | Story clicks & Demo visits | Native student social media channel |
| Vendor Notice Boards | Canteen Stall Staff | Streamline incoming orders without manual calls | View Vendor Portal | Admin logins & Queue updates | Direct contact point for canteen vendors |

## Launch Sequence
1. Prepare promotional landing page (`/landing-page/index.html`) and live web prototype link.
2. Place QR code flyers near Building 11 classroom doors and Building 6 canteen tables.
3. Share promotional announcements in RSU student LINE official groups.
4. Track visitor clicks, cart conversions, and cycle times in `/docs/acquisition-metrics.md`.
5. Collect user feedback via prototype testing notes and refine menu filtering and checkout UX.

## Risk and Mitigation
* **Risk:** High peak-hour order volume overloading canteen vendor preparation capacity.
* **Mitigation:** Cap incoming orders to 8 active orders per vendor queue and display realistic prep time badges (`12 min`) on vendor cards.
